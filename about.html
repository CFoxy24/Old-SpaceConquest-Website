◆ ABOUT ◆

EXPLORE DIFFERENT STAR SYSTEMS

Explore a large variety of procedurally generated solar systems, discover what the lore of SpaceConquest can offer to you. Maybe you want more adventures? You can complete differents puzzles and quests scattered all over abandonned stations, ship wrecks, and the entire universe.

Seems boring? Don't worry the game is planned to have MANY ways to have fun exploring, to variety of gameplay, from the most serious ways to the most ridiculous ways! And you can play with your friends if you want!

FIGHT FOR YOUR FACTION

Fight is a key feature in gameplay, that's why alot of the work has been made towards having the best fight experience you can possibly get on Roblox. You can fight for yourself, for your faction, for your friends, with players, bots and other factions!

Not your thing? Fight can be avoided if you really want peace, you always have the possibility to flee from ennemies without serious repercussions.

MINE VARIOUS ASTEROIDS

If you want to farm, here is mining! But wait, it is not the kind of mining that you think, I tried to make the least boring mining you can find in space games. You will not "just mine and sell", it comes with many different ways to farm, from the most effective, to the funniest mining you could get in this kind of game!
